## Platform

web

## Users

Strangers who scan a QR — off Iddo's t-shirt, off any printed document at the final
exhibition, or later from portfolio material. Mobile-first by definition (the scan happens
on a phone). Mixed Hebrew/English audience: crit-day jury and visitors (TAU, Hebrew) now;
M.Arch admissions readers, employers, and internet strangers later. Confirmed: the hub is
the project's PERMANENT public front — crit day (2026-08-09) is its launch, not its life.

## Product Purpose

The single front door to POETIKS (פה־אתיקה), Iddo Naim's B.Arch final project at TAU.
Success action (confirmed): the visitor UNDERSTANDS what the project is. Links are
secondary to explanation — they leave able to say what POETIKS is, then follow a thread
into it (gallery, studio app, thesis text, contact).

## Positioning

POETIKS treats architectural meaning-making through internet humor: a meme platform for
architecture → an AI cuboid studio generating seventy variations of a cube → an
architectural return to a real field/site. The mechanism no neighbor can copy: the project
built its own working artifacts (live meme gallery, live generative studio app, animated
project map, a mapped world with named districts — PLATFORM, CUBOID STUDIO, THEORY,
THE SEVENTY) rather than describing them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the working thing, never a description of it — the project's proof is its artifacts.
2. Humor is method, not decoration: the surface may be funny the way the thesis is funny.
3. One world, many doors: hub explains first, routes second.
4. Every printed object is an entry point (`/x/<doc>`) — the hub is an explainer system,
   not just a landing page.
5. Permanent front: nothing crit-day-disposable in the core design.
